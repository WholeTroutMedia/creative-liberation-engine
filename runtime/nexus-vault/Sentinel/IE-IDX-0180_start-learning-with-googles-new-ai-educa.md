## 🧠 ATHENA Directive

> [!tip] Primary Directive
> Establish the Creative Liberation Engine as the definitive, self-sovereign platform for AI literacy and practical application, offering modular, high-fidelity educational experiences and seamless integration of learned AI principles into agent orchestration.

### Rationale

The Google AI Educator Series highlights a critical need for accessible, modular AI education. By architecting a native AI literacy framework within the Creative Liberation Engine, we uphold Article I (Sovereignty) and Article IV (Quality Standards), ensuring complete control over content, user experience, and direct applicability to agent orchestration. This approach transforms external initiatives into opportunities for internal enhancement and platform leadership.

## ⚡ Strategic Options

### ✅ Creative Liberation Engine AI Literacy Module (Internal Focus)

Develop a native, self-sovereign AI literacy module directly within the Creative Liberation Engine. This module will offer 'snackable' micro-trainings and 'stackable' learning paths, mirroring the Google series' pedagogical approach but tailored specifically for AI agent orchestration and the Creative Liberation Engine ecosystem. Content will be curated and developed internally, ensuring deep integration with existing and future Creative Liberation Engine features.

> **Tradeoffs:** High initial investment in content creation and platform development. However, it provides maximum control over quality, curriculum, and direct applicability to Creative Liberation Engine users. Ensures complete data sovereignty and a bespoke learning experience.
> **Recommendation:** `PREFERRED`

### 🟡 Federated AI Education Gateway (Integration Focus)

Create an integration layer within the Creative Liberation Engine that can aggregate and present educational content from external AI literacy series, including Google's, while providing a unified Creative Liberation Engine user experience. This acts as a 'smart portal' that curates relevant external resources, translating them into an accessible format.

> **Tradeoffs:** Relies on external content availability and quality, leading to less control over the curriculum. Lower initial content creation cost, but higher complexity in maintaining external API integrations and content parsing. Potential for external content to become outdated or unavailable.
> **Recommendation:** `VIABLE`

### 🟡 Open-Source AI Education Contribution & Export (Community Focus)

Architect the Creative Liberation Engine to not only consume but also contribute to and export open-source AI education content. This involves defining a standard for modular AI learning units and building tools within the Creative Liberation Engine to create, manage, and publish these units to external open-source repositories, while also allowing the Creative Liberation Engine to seamlessly import and display such content.

> **Tradeoffs:** Requires significant effort in standardization, community engagement, and maintenance of open-source projects. Benefits from community collaboration, wider impact, and potential for external contributions to enrich the Creative Liberation Engine's own learning materials. Less immediate self-sovereignty over content, but builds strategic influence.
> **Recommendation:** `VIABLE`
