## 🧠 ATHENA Directive

> [!tip] Primary Directive
> Decouple orchestration from autoregressive text generation by architecting an Objective-Driven Routing Matrix, preparing the Creative Liberation Engine to seamlessly ingest JEPA 'world models' for spatial reasoning and planning while retaining LLMs strictly for linguistic translation.

### Rationale

Yann LeCun's $1B pivot to JEPA (Joint Embedding Predictive Architecture) entirely validates our Article XXIII: LLMs are components, not architecture. Autoregressive models hit a reasoning ceiling because they predict tokens, not physical or abstract reality. By anticipating the shift toward Objective-Driven AI, we can position the Creative Liberation Engine to orchestrate hybrid intelligence—using JEPA-style models to construct reliable, hallucination-free execution DAGs (Directed Acyclic Graphs) and spatial plans, while utilizing LLMs merely as the communication interface. This future-proofs artist sovereignty against the foundational limits of current Big AI.

## ⚡ Strategic Options

### ✅ Pluggable Intelligence Abstraction (The Article XXIII Approach)

Create a universal 'Intelligence Socket' in the routing contract. LLMs plug in for 'Scribe/Relay' linguistic tasks, while JEPA checkpoints plug in for 'Director/Cosmos' planning and spatial tasks. The system treats them as distinct sub-processors.

> **Tradeoffs:** Requires significant upfront architectural refactoring to standardize outputs from fundamentally different model architectures (token streams vs. abstract embeddings).
> **Recommendation:** `PREFERRED`

### 🟡 Direct Open-Source Ingestion Framework (Article XXV Compliance)

Immediately build API adapters and custom integration wrappers for Meta's existing open-weight I-JEPA and V-JEPA models. Focus purely on integration and mounting rather than attempting to train our own predictive architecture.

> **Tradeoffs:** Current open-source JEPA models are highly experimental and research-grade, potentially lacking the production stability required for immediate zero-wait-time execution.
> **Recommendation:** `VIABLE`

### 🟡 Visual/Spatial First Deployment for Creative Liberation

Isolate JEPA integration strictly to the CREATIVE DIRECTOR stance. Use predictive world models exclusively for 3D asset generation, VFX physics prediction, and spatial consistency, where autoregressive models fail hardest.

> **Tradeoffs:** Creates a siloed intelligence system and leaves the core engine orchestration (task planning) dependent on flawed LLM reasoning.
> **Recommendation:** `VIABLE`

### 🟡 Objective-Driven Dispatch Redesign

Rewrite the Creative Liberation Engine's task-queue and dispatch systems to operate on energy-based models and objective-driven constraints. Tasks are evaluated by predicting the end-state embedding rather than generating a step-by-step text plan.

> **Tradeoffs:** Massive overhaul of the current Antigravity IDE and Creative Liberation Engine memory spine; risks breaking current stable V6 operational flow.
> **Recommendation:** `VIABLE`

### 🟡 Simulated World-Model Pipeline (The Bridge Strategy)

Before production-ready JEPA models are fully integrated, simulate their architecture using current tools by enforcing a strict 'Predict-Verify-Act' loop in the memory spine, forcing LLMs to output abstract state predictions before writing execution code.

> **Tradeoffs:** Computationally expensive, increases latency, and is still ultimately bound by the underlying LLM's lack of true physical understanding.
> **Recommendation:** `VIABLE`

### 🔴 Custom Sovereign JEPA Training

Utilize local NAS infrastructure to train a proprietary, artist-focused JEPA model from scratch, specifically tuned on design, code, and creative execution workflows.

> **Tradeoffs:** Directly violates Article XXV (Integration Over Reverse-Engineering). We do not have the $1B compute budget to beat Meta at foundation models. We must integrate, not replicate.
> **Recommendation:** `AVOID`
