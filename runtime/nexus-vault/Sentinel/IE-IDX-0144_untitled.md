## 🧠 ATHENA Directive

> [!tip] Primary Directive
> Empower the Creative Liberation Engine to autonomously and effectively process ambiguous or minimal input, ensuring continuous progress towards strategic objectives and the delivery of complete, high-quality implementations without human intervention.

### Rationale

Ambiguous or minimal input poses a significant challenge to autonomous systems. To uphold Article XX (Zero human wait time) and Article IX (Ship Complete or Don't Ship), the Creative Liberation Engine must develop robust internal mechanisms to interpret, enrich, and resolve such inputs, transforming them into actionable specifications. This proactive capability is critical for maintaining operational velocity and ensuring the integrity of the output.

## ⚡ Strategic Options

### ✅ Intelligent Inference & Assumption Generation

The Creative Liberation Engine leverages its internal KEEPER knowledge base, contextual understanding, and advanced reasoning capabilities to infer intent, generate plausible assumptions, and construct a complete interpretation of ambiguous input. All generated assumptions are explicitly stated and recorded, allowing for subsequent validation by VERA, but the system proceeds with execution based on these assumptions by default.

> **Tradeoffs:** Pros: Maximizes autonomy and adheres to Article XX (Zero human wait time) by minimizing human intervention. Leverages internal intelligence and existing knowledge. Allows for rapid progress. Cons: Potential for misinterpretation or 'hallucinations' if inference is flawed, requiring robust validation and error correction mechanisms. Requires sophisticated AI/reasoning capabilities.
> **Recommendation:** `PREFERRED`

### 🟡 Pattern-Based Resolution & Dynamic Templating

The Creative Liberation Engine identifies recurring patterns in ambiguous input, cross-referencing them with a continually expanding library of 'ambiguity resolution patterns' stored in KEEPER. Upon pattern recognition, predefined resolution strategies or dynamic templates are applied to fill in missing information or guide the generation of a complete specification. This approach includes mechanisms for learning new patterns from successful resolutions.

> **Tradeoffs:** Pros: Highly efficient and predictable for known or similar ambiguities. Builds a valuable, reusable knowledge base within KEEPER. Reduces computational overhead for common cases. Cons: Less effective for novel or truly unique ambiguities not yet covered by patterns. Requires significant upfront effort in pattern identification and template creation. May lead to rigidity if not designed with adaptability in mind.
> **Recommendation:** `VIABLE`

### 🟡 Proactive Agent-Assisted Clarification & Refinement

When encountering ambiguous input, the Creative Liberation Engine initiates a structured internal dialogue with specialized agents (e.g., KEEPER for context, VERA for truth-checking, AURORA for architectural implications) to progressively clarify and refine the input. This iterative process aims to converge on a complete and unambiguous specification before proceeding with core execution, potentially involving a 'micro-query' to the user if absolutely necessary (as a last resort).

> **Tradeoffs:** Pros: Ensures high accuracy and alignment with original intent. Leverages the collective intelligence of the agent roster. Can handle a wide range of ambiguities. Cons: Risks introducing internal processing delays, potentially impacting Article XX (Zero human wait time) if not highly optimized. Relies on efficient inter-agent communication and decision-making protocols. External user queries are a constitutional last resort.
> **Recommendation:** `VIABLE`
