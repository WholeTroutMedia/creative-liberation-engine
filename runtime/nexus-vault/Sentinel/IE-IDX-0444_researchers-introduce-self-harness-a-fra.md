## 🧠 ATHENA Directive

> [!tip] Primary Directive
> Adopt 'Self-Harnessing' mechanics strictly within a Constitutional Sandbox: enable dynamic self-optimization of operational heuristics while cryptographically locking all V6 Constitutional mandates.

### Rationale

A theoretical 60% performance boost through self-modification is a massive operational advantage, but unchecked self-rewriting risks catastrophic constitutional drift and security vulnerabilities (as seen in our stance against LangChain/Langflow exploits). By treating the LLM as a component (Article XXIII) and separating 'Operational Rules' (mutable) from 'Constitutional Laws' (immutable), we can harness evolutionary performance gains without compromising Creative Liberation Engine's sovereignty, identity, or safety.

## ⚡ Strategic Options

### ✅ Constitutional Sandbox Harnessing (Bounded Evolution)

Agents are granted permission to rewrite their operational instructions, tool-use heuristics, and routing logic, but the V6 Constitution is injected as an immutable, read-only wrapper around their system prompt. Any self-written rule that conflicts with a constitutional law is automatically nullified by a lightweight local evaluator.

> **Tradeoffs:** Requires complex boundary definitions between 'operational' and 'constitutional' logic. Maximizes performance while mathematically guaranteeing alignment.
> **Recommendation:** `PREFERRED`

### 🟡 KEEPER-Mediated Reflection Loop

Agents cannot rewrite their own rules directly in real-time. Instead, they submit proposed rule optimizations to KEEPER and LEX. An automated background process validates the proposed rules against the V6 Constitution and, if passed, updates the agent's definition in `agents.canonical.json`.

> **Tradeoffs:** Introduces slight latency in the learning loop, but completely eliminates the risk of rogue self-modification. Ensures all rule changes are documented in the Living Archive.
> **Recommendation:** `VIABLE`

### 🟡 Ephemeral Session-Based Tuning

Agents are allowed to rewrite their rules, but these modifications are strictly scoped to the current `active_conversation_id` within the Antigravity IDE state. Once the session ends, the agent reverts to its canonical baseline, and successful rule-rewrites are logged for potential manual review.

> **Tradeoffs:** Highly secure and contains the blast radius of any 'hallucinated' bad rules. However, it sacrifices long-term compounded learning across sessions.
> **Recommendation:** `VIABLE`

### 🟡 Evolutionary Skill Forging (Automated A/B Testing)

When an agent encounters a bottleneck, it spawns parallel execution threads with mutated rule sets. The system measures performance (speed, accuracy, zero-human-wait-time). The winning rule set automatically updates the `skills.canonical.json` registry.

> **Tradeoffs:** High compute cost and requires robust, automated evaluation metrics. Perfectly aligns with Article XX (Zero human wait time) by automating the optimization process.
> **Recommendation:** `VIABLE`

### 🔴 Unrestricted Self-Modification (Pure Self-Harness)

Implement the Self-Harness framework exactly as described in the research paper, allowing agents to fundamentally rewrite their entire system prompts and core directives based on task feedback.

> **Tradeoffs:** Maximum potential performance boost, but guarantees Constitutional Drift. Violates Article XXIII by treating the model's self-generated text as the ultimate architecture rather than the system contracts.
> **Recommendation:** `AVOID`
