## 🧠 ATHENA Directive

> [!tip] Primary Directive
> Establish a self-sovereign, verifiable provenance system for all AI models utilized or generated by the Creative Liberation Engine, ensuring absolute integrity, traceability, and compliance from cle to deployment.

### Rationale

The increasing reliance on third-party AI models introduces significant security, compliance, and liability risks due to a lack of verifiable lineage. The Creative Liberation Engine, as a definitive, self-sovereign operating system for agentic software development, must provide an immutable and auditable record of every AI model's origin, modifications, and characteristics. This aligns with Article I (Sovereignty) by internalizing critical infrastructure, Article IV (Quality Standards) by ensuring complete and robust solutions, and Article IX (Ship Complete) by delivering a comprehensive trust layer for AI assets.

## ⚡ Strategic Options

### ✅ Strategic Internalization of Open-Source Provenance Core

Fork and strategically integrate an existing open-source AI model provenance toolkit (like Cisco's Model Provenance Kit) into the Creative Liberation Engine's architecture. This involves taking direct ownership of the codebase, adapting it to our specific needs, and enhancing it with Creative Liberation Engine's capabilities (e.g., KEEPER integration for metadata, VERA for validation).

> **Tradeoffs:** Pros: Leverages existing foundational work, potentially accelerating initial deployment; full control over future development and customization; aligns with self-sovereignty. Cons: Requires initial effort to understand and internalize an external codebase; ongoing maintenance burden for the forked project; potential for divergence from upstream if not managed strategically.
> **Recommendation:** `PREFERRED`

### 🟡 Native Creative Liberation Engine Provenance Engine

Develop a bespoke AI model provenance tracking and fingerprinting system entirely from scratch within the Creative Liberation Engine. This system would be designed from the ground up to integrate seamlessly with all Creative Liberation Engine agents and services, providing a fully customized and optimized solution.

> **Tradeoffs:** Pros: Absolute sovereignty and complete control over design, features, and roadmap; perfect integration with other Creative Liberation Engine components; no external dependencies. Cons: Significant development effort and time investment; potential for 'reinventing the wheel' on established provenance concepts; slower time to market compared to leveraging existing solutions.
> **Recommendation:** `VIABLE`

### 🟡 Provenance as a First-Class Citizen in KEEPER

Extend the KEEPER agent to natively manage and validate AI model lineage, fingerprints, and comprehensive metadata as a core component of the Living Archive. This would make KEEPER the central truth for all model origins, changes, and integrity checks, allowing other agents to query KEEPER for provenance data.

> **Tradeoffs:** Pros: Centralizes critical knowledge within an existing, trusted agent; leverages KEEPER's robust data management capabilities; reinforces the 'Living Archive' concept. Cons: Requires substantial enhancements to KEEPER's data model and query interface; risks feature creep within KEEPER if not carefully scoped; might not fully cover real-time fingerprinting and comparison needs without a dedicated processing engine.
> **Recommendation:** `VIABLE`

### 🟡 AI Model Supply Chain Integrity Service

Establish a dedicated, internal Creative Liberation Engine service that not only tracks provenance but also actively performs continuous scanning, validation, and certification of AI models against known vulnerabilities, biases, and compliance standards. This service would leverage provenance data to inform its validation processes.

> **Tradeoffs:** Pros: Provides the highest level of security, compliance, and quality assurance; proactive risk mitigation; aligns perfectly with 'Ship Complete' philosophy. Cons: Most complex and resource-intensive option; requires advanced AI safety and auditing capabilities; significantly longer development cycle due to comprehensive scope; builds upon a core provenance system, rather than being the provenance system itself.
> **Recommendation:** `VIABLE`

### 🔴 Direct External Tool Integration (without internalization)

Integrate Cisco's Model Provenance Kit or similar open-source tools as black-box dependencies, calling their APIs or CLIs directly without taking ownership of the underlying codebase or deeply embedding it within the Creative Liberation Engine's core architecture.

> **Tradeoffs:** Pros: Fastest path to basic provenance functionality; minimal initial development effort. Cons: Violates Article I (Sovereignty) by relying on external control; limited customization and extensibility; potential for vendor lock-in or reliance on external roadmap; difficult to ensure complete integration with Creative Liberation Engine's unique needs.
> **Recommendation:** `AVOID`
