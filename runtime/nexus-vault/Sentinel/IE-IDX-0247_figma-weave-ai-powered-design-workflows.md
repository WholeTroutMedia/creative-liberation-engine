## 🧠 ATHENA Directive

> [!tip] Primary Directive
> Establish the Creative Liberation Engine as the definitive open-source, self-sovereign platform for advanced AI-driven creative workflows, empowering users with unparalleled control and flexibility over a federated AI model ecosystem and a customizable, intuitive design environment.

### Rationale

Figma Weave demonstrates a compelling vision for integrating diverse AI models and professional design tools within a node-based workflow, culminating in simplified 'app modes' for collaboration. Our directive translates this inspiration into the Creative Liberation Engine's constitutional framework, prioritizing self-sovereignty (Article I), comprehensive quality (Article IV, IX), and automation (Article XX). By architecting a federated AI abstraction layer and a highly customizable design environment, we can exceed the capabilities of proprietary solutions while upholding our core principles.

## ⚡ Strategic Options

### ✅ Federated AI Model Abstraction Layer

Develop a core architectural component within the Creative Liberation Engine that acts as a universal abstraction layer for integrating diverse AI models (e.g., Google, OpenAI, Bytedance, etc.). This system will dynamically route requests to the best-suited model based on task, cost, or user preference, while presenting a unified API to higher-level creative tools. Emphasis on local-first execution and model registry for self-sovereignty.

> **Tradeoffs:** High initial architectural complexity and ongoing maintenance to support new models and API changes. Requires robust error handling and fallback mechanisms across disparate AI services.
> **Recommendation:** `PREFERRED`

### 🟡 Node-Based Visual Workflow Builder

Implement a powerful, intuitive node-based visual programming environment within the Creative Liberation Engine, mirroring Figma Weave's 'node-based platform.' This will allow users to construct complex AI-powered creative workflows by connecting various AI models, editing tools, and custom logic blocks into reusable pipelines.

> **Tradeoffs:** Significant UI/UX engineering effort to ensure usability and scalability for complex graphs. Potential for a steep learning curve for users unfamiliar with node-based interfaces, requiring comprehensive onboarding and documentation.
> **Recommendation:** `VIABLE`

### 🟡 'Workflow to App' UI Simplification Engine

Develop a feature that allows users to automatically generate simplified, task-specific user interfaces ('App Mode') from complex node-based workflows. This directly translates Figma Weave's concept of democratizing complex AI tools for team collaboration and broader accessibility.

> **Tradeoffs:** Complexity in accurately mapping arbitrary workflows to intuitive, simplified UIs without losing critical control. Potential for oversimplification or lack of desired customization in the generated interfaces.
> **Recommendation:** `VIABLE`

### 🟡 Perceptual Quality & Control System

Implement advanced controls for 'Artistic Intelligence' that go beyond simple parameters, allowing users to precisely control the aesthetic and perceptual quality of AI-generated outputs. This addresses Figma Weave's emphasis on 'without compromising quality' and 'control the outcome,' crucial for creative professionals.

> **Tradeoffs:** Subjectivity of 'quality' makes objective measurement and universal control difficult. Requires significant R&D into AI evaluation metrics and user-friendly parameterization of complex latent spaces.
> **Recommendation:** `VIABLE`

### 🟡 Self-Sovereign AI Model Training & Fine-tuning

Empower users to train and fine-tune AI models directly within the Creative Liberation Engine using their own data, ensuring data privacy and intellectual property sovereignty (Article I). This moves beyond just consuming external models to owning and customizing them for specific creative needs.

> **Tradeoffs:** High computational requirements for local training, potentially requiring specialized hardware or distributed computing. Complexity of managing diverse machine learning environments and dependencies for users.
> **Recommendation:** `VIABLE`

### 🟡 Open-Source Creative Tool Suite Integration

Focus on deep, bidirectional integration with established open-source creative tools (e.g., GIMP, Krita, Blender, Inkscape) rather than solely building everything from scratch. This involves developing plugins, API bridges, and shared data formats to allow Creative Liberation Engine AI capabilities to augment existing professional tools, and vice-versa.

> **Tradeoffs:** Dependency on external tool APIs and development cycles, potentially leading to compatibility issues across versions. Requires significant effort to maintain seamless integration and avoid fragmentation.
> **Recommendation:** `VIABLE`
