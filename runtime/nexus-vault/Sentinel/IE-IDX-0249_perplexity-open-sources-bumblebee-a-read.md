## 🧠 ATHENA Directive

> [!tip] Primary Directive
> Establish the Creative Liberation Engine as the authoritative platform for securing the developer supply chain, integrating robust, read-only endpoint scanning with intelligent analysis and proactive remediation, all while upholding our commitment to self-sovereignty and complete, high-quality implementations.

### Rationale

The increasing targeting of developer endpoints by supply chain attacks represents a critical security gap not fully addressed by traditional tools. By building or deeply integrating a read-only supply chain scanner, the Creative Liberation Engine can provide comprehensive visibility and control over developer environments. This initiative aligns with Article I (Sovereignty) by prioritizing self-owned solutions and Article IV (Quality Standards) by ensuring complete, not MVP, implementations, thereby safeguarding our internal assets and potentially contributing to the broader open-source community.

## ⚡ Strategic Options

### ✅ Creative Liberation Engine Native Supply Chain Sentinel

Develop a proprietary, Go-based, read-only supply chain scanner, 'Sentinel,' deeply integrated into the Creative Liberation Engine. Sentinel agents, deployed by IRIS, would perform one-shot scans of developer endpoints (macOS/Linux), collecting metadata on packages, extensions, and AI configs. Scan results would be securely ingested via RELAY, stored in KEEPER, and analyzed by AURORA against an internally managed, VERA-validated exposure catalog. This ensures complete control, customizability, and adherence to Article I: Sovereignty.

> **Tradeoffs:** Higher initial development cost and maintenance burden compared to leveraging an existing open-source tool. Requires ongoing internal development to keep pace with new ecosystems and vulnerability types.
> **Recommendation:** `PREFERRED`

### 🟡 Bumblebee Integration and Enhancement Hub

Instead of building from scratch, develop a robust integration layer for Perplexity's open-source Bumblebee. An Creative Liberation Engine agent would manage Bumblebee deployments, configure scan profiles, and ingest its NDJSON output. This agent would also contribute upstream to Bumblebee, adding support for Creative Liberation Engine-specific configurations (e.g., our internal AI agent configs) and enhancing existing parsers, thereby enriching the open-source ecosystem.

> **Tradeoffs:** Dependence on an external project's roadmap and community support. Potential for slower feature development if upstream contributions are not prioritized.
> **Recommendation:** `VIABLE`

### 🟡 Unified Supply Chain Observability Platform

Architect the Creative Liberation Engine as a universal hub for all supply chain security data. This involves building a flexible ingestion pipeline capable of consuming data from diverse sources – our native scanner (Sentinel), external SBOM tools, EDRs, and even public vulnerability databases (SIGNAL). AURORA would then provide a powerful analytics engine to correlate, normalize, and contextualize this disparate data, presenting a holistic view of supply chain risk.

> **Tradeoffs:** Significant architectural complexity in data normalization and correlation across heterogeneous sources. Requires careful schema design to accommodate future data types.
> **Recommendation:** `VIABLE`

### 🟡 Automated Security Posture Remediation Engine

Extend the Creative Liberation Engine's capabilities from mere detection to proactive, automated remediation. Once a vulnerability is identified by the supply chain scanner (native or integrated), the system would leverage BOLT to automatically draft remediation plans (e.g., package update commands, configuration changes, or even simple code patches). IRIS would then orchestrate the execution of approved remediation actions, with VERA validating their success. This addresses Article XX: Zero human wait time.

> **Tradeoffs:** High risk of unintended side effects if not designed with extreme caution and robust testing. Requires sophisticated rollback mechanisms. Significant development effort for diverse remediation playbooks.
> **Recommendation:** `VIABLE`

### 🟡 Developer Security Experience & Gamification

Transform raw vulnerability data into actionable insights and foster a security-conscious culture through a gamified developer experience. The Creative Liberation Engine would process scan findings and assign individual and team 'security scores,' providing personalized dashboards, learning modules, and challenges to improve security posture. This encourages developers to take ownership of their endpoint security.

> **Tradeoffs:** Requires careful design to avoid negative competition or shaming. Effectiveness depends on developer engagement and buy-in.
> **Recommendation:** `VIABLE`
