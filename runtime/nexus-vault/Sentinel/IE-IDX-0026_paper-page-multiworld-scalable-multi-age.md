## 🧠 ATHENA Directive

> [!tip] Primary Directive
> The Creative Liberation Engine shall develop and integrate a self-hosted, scalable multi-agent, multi-view video world model, inspired by MultiWorld, to serve as its foundational simulation and training environment, ensuring complete control over its operational parameters and data generation capabilities.

### Rationale

To achieve true autonomy and self-improvement, the Creative Liberation Engine requires a robust, self-owned simulation environment that can accurately model complex multi-agent interactions and diverse perspectives. This aligns with Article I (Sovereignty) by avoiding reliance on external, proprietary systems. By aiming for a complete implementation (Article IV), we ensure a high-quality, production-ready system from the outset, enabling automated agent training and testing, thus supporting Article XX (Zero human wait time).

## ⚡ Strategic Options

### ✅ Creative Liberation Engine's Sovereign World Model

Develop a core, self-hosted world model within the Creative Liberation Engine, directly implementing and advancing the principles of MultiWorld (scalable multi-agent, multi-view, action-controllable, multi-view-consistent video generation). This model will be the authoritative source for simulating complex environments and agent interactions for all Creative Liberation Engine operations, including training, validation, and hypothetical scenario generation.

> **Tradeoffs:** Requires significant initial investment in research, development, and computational resources. However, it provides complete control, customization, and long-term strategic advantage by owning the foundational simulation layer.
> **Recommendation:** `PREFERRED`

### 🟡 Modular Simulation Core for Agent Experimentation

Construct a modular simulation framework capable of integrating various world model components, including those inspired by MultiWorld. This approach focuses on creating a flexible architecture where different world models, agent policies, and rendering techniques can be swapped and experimented with, ensuring the system can evolve with future research.

> **Tradeoffs:** Potentially slower to reach a fully integrated, high-fidelity world model compared to a direct implementation. Offers superior adaptability and extensibility for future research directions.
> **Recommendation:** `VIABLE`

### 🟡 Advanced Agent Identity & Latent Space Coherence

Prioritize the development of a dedicated sub-system focused on robust agent identity encoding and maintaining action-controllable coherence within shared latent spaces, drawing heavily from MultiWorld's AIE (Agent Identity Embeddings) concepts. This component would ensure distinct yet interactive agent behaviors across multi-view scenarios, serving as a critical foundation for any multi-agent world model.

> **Tradeoffs:** Addresses only a specific, albeit crucial, aspect of a full world model. Requires seamless integration with other components responsible for video generation and physics.
> **Recommendation:** `VIABLE`

### 🟡 Synthetic Data Generation for Creative Liberation Engine Perception

Implement a specialized module inspired by MultiWorld's video generation capabilities to produce highly realistic, action-controllable, multi-view synthetic video datasets. This data would be primarily used to train and validate Creative Liberation Engine's perception and vision systems, reducing reliance on costly real-world data collection.

> **Tradeoffs:** Focuses solely on data generation, not a full interactive simulation environment for agents. While invaluable for perception, it doesn't directly provide a dynamic interaction space for training agent policies.
> **Recommendation:** `VIABLE`

### 🔴 External Engine Integration for Rapid Prototyping

Explore and integrate with existing high-fidelity game engines or simulation platforms (e.g., Unity, Unreal Engine) to rapidly prototype multi-agent, multi-view environments. MultiWorld's principles would guide the design of agent control and view consistency within these external platforms.

> **Tradeoffs:** Violates Article I (Sovereignty) by relying on third-party solutions. While faster for initial prototyping, it introduces dependencies and limits long-term control and deep customization.
> **Recommendation:** `AVOID`
