Emphasize that the Creative Liberation Engine's 'Open Design' enables unparalleled performance optimization and deep customization because all architectural layers are transparent and modifiable. > **Tradeoffs:** Pros: Strong appeal to performance-critical applications and highly specialized agent deployments, clear technical superiority narrative, positions Creative Liberation Engine as the 'power user' and 'enterprise-grade' choice. Cons: Requires rigorous security practices, a robust and responsive vulnerability disclosure process, and constant community vigilance; may not be perceived as a primary 'feature' by all users compared to raw performance or new capabilities.
