## 🧠 ATHENA Directive

> [!tip] Primary Directive
> Establish the Creative Liberation Engine as the definitive Sovereign Agent Identity Provider (IdP), proving that true AI agency requires cryptographic autonomy, not rented enterprise SaaS credentials.

### Rationale

NewCore's $66M funding validates our Article XXIII thesis: the system, not the LLM, provides the persistent architecture of identity. However, renting agent identity from a VC-backed SaaS violates Article I (Sovereignty). We must map this market signal into a catalyst for our own sovereign identity spine. We will elevate our existing `AgentIdentity` framework into a cryptographically secure, zero-trust, locally hosted identity layer that allows our agents to act as verified entities without external dependencies, while remaining interoperable with external enterprise networks via Article XXV.

## ⚡ Strategic Options

### ✅ Sovereign Cryptographic Agent Identity (IdP) Spine

Elevate the existing `agents.canonical.json` into a full local Identity Provider. Generate localized cryptographic keypairs for every agent (ATHENA, BOLT, LEX, etc.) stored securely on the NAS. Agents sign their commits, API requests, and handoffs, establishing undeniable, sovereign provenance for all actions.

> **Tradeoffs:** Requires significant upfront architectural work by AURORA and custom auth middleware for the dispatch system, but guarantees 100% compliance with Article I and provides absolute security.
> **Recommendation:** `PREFERRED`

### 🟡 Zero-Trust Constitutional Identity Verification

Treat identity not as a static profile, but as a continuous state of constitutional alignment. Bind the agent's active identity token to the `ConstitutionalGuard`. If an agent's output drifts from its constitutional mandate, its identity and access rights are instantly revoked by LEX until re-aligned.

> **Tradeoffs:** Introduces high computational overhead and potential latency due to continuous monitoring, but ensures agents cannot go rogue while authenticated.
> **Recommendation:** `VIABLE`

### 🟡 Enterprise Identity Adapter (NewCore/Okta Bridge)

In adherence to Article XXV (Integration Over Reverse-Engineering), build a `SIGNAL` wrapper that allows our sovereign agents to securely authenticate into external enterprise environments (like NewCore or Okta) when required for B2B tasks, without storing our root identities there.

> **Tradeoffs:** Creates a surface area that depends on external API schemas, but allows Creative Liberation Engine agents to be deployed into corporate environments seamlessly.
> **Recommendation:** `VIABLE`

### 🟡 Agent 'HR', RBAC & Resource Allocation Protocol

Expand the `AgentIdentity` interface to treat agents as managed digital employees. Implement Role-Based Access Control (RBAC), token budget limits, compute quotas, and structured onboarding/offboarding protocols within the Hive architecture.

> **Tradeoffs:** Adds bureaucratic complexity to the dispatch system and task queues, but prevents runaway compute costs and tightly controls scope.
> **Recommendation:** `VIABLE`

### 🟡 Federated Agent Identity Network (Open Protocol)

Design our identity schema around open Decentralized Identifier (DID) standards. Allow Creative Liberation Engine instances to securely identify and verify their agents to other sovereign AI networks globally, creating an open-source counter-movement to centralized platforms.

> **Tradeoffs:** Complex to implement and relies on the broader adoption of open standards, but perfectly aligns with our mission of artist liberation and open protocols.
> **Recommendation:** `VIABLE`

### 🔴 Outsource Internal Identity to NewCore API

Migrate our internal agent registry to NewCore's platform to leverage their VC-funded R&D and out-of-the-box identity management features.

> **Tradeoffs:** Catastrophic violation of Article I (Sovereignty) and Article XXIII. Surrenders control of our core architecture to a third party.
> **Recommendation:** `AVOID`
