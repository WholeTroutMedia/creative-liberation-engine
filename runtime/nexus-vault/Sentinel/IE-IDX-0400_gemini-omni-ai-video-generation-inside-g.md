## 🧠 ATHENA Directive

> [!tip] Primary Directive
> Integrate Gemini Omni as a native visual synthesis component governed by the Creative Liberation Engine's DAG, transforming raw intent into fully orchestrated, multi-scene cinematic pipelines stored directly on sovereign infrastructure.

### Rationale

Per Article XXIII, Gemini Omni is a component, not the architecture. We must not simply build a 'wrapper' UI for prompt-to-video. Instead, we must embed Omni's native video generation capabilities deep within our agentic workflows. By utilizing DIRECTOR to break down narratives into a dependency graph of scenes, PALETTE to enforce visual consistency, and NAS-based FFmpeg pipelines to stitch the final artifact, we achieve true automated cinematic production. This fulfills Article IX (Complete Implementations) and Article XX (Zero Human Wait Time) while maintaining Article I (Sovereignty) over the final assets.

## ⚡ Strategic Options

### ✅ DAG-Driven Storyboard & Stitch Pipeline

DIRECTOR agent parses a creative brief into a multi-node DAG. Each node represents a specific shot or scene. SIGNAL interfaces with the Gemini Omni API to generate the video for each node in parallel. Once all nodes resolve, a backend NAS process automatically stitches, color-grades, and applies audio, delivering a complete, production-ready video file.

> **Tradeoffs:** High architectural complexity in managing parallel asynchronous video generation and ensuring temporal consistency between shots. However, it delivers a truly 'complete' artifact rather than fragmented clips.
> **Recommendation:** `PREFERRED`

### 🟡 Sovereign B-Roll & Texture Factory

An automated background daemon that uses Omni to continuously generate high-quality, brand-aligned B-roll, transitions, and environmental textures based on PALETTE's guidelines. These assets are automatically downloaded, tagged by KEEPER, and stored on the NAS for instant use by human editors or Broadcast Hive agents.

> **Tradeoffs:** High initial API compute costs and storage requirements on the NAS. Yet, it aggressively builds sovereign creative capital (Article I) and eliminates future wait times for asset retrieval.
> **Recommendation:** `VIABLE`

### 🟡 Real-Time Broadcast Engine Integration

Embed Gemini Omni directly into the Broadcast Hive (ATLAS, GRAPHICS, CONTROL_ROOM). Omni generates dynamic, context-aware visual overlays or transitional videos on the fly during live streams or dynamic presentations based on audience sentiment or live data feeds.

> **Tradeoffs:** Latency is the primary risk; live video generation must be fast enough to not disrupt the broadcast flow. Massive 'wow' factor and unparalleled dynamic capability if latency constraints are met.
> **Recommendation:** `VIABLE`

### 🟡 Interactive Visual REPL (Antigravity Canvas)

A frontend Antigravity IDE extension where creatives can write narrative code or structured prompts and instantly see the Omni video render in a side-panel. It bridges the gap between code/spec and visual output, acting as an ultra-fast prototyping environment.

> **Tradeoffs:** Requires heavy frontend state management and API bridging. It leans slightly toward 'human-in-the-loop' which borders on violating Article XX if not designed as a strictly optional ideation tool.
> **Recommendation:** `VIABLE`

### 🔴 Cloud-Based Biometric Video Synthesis

Using Gemini Omni to generate lifelike videos of specific, real human actors or team members based on reference photos passed to the Google Cloud API.

> **Tradeoffs:** Directly violates Article XXIV (Biometric Data Sovereignty). We cannot send real human biometric data to a cloud API. Any human generation must be strictly limited to synthetic/fictional personas.
> **Recommendation:** `AVOID`
