## 🧠 ATHENA Directive

> [!tip] Primary Directive
> Architect and specify the Creative Liberation Engine's Recursive Self-Improvement (RSI) Orchestration Layer, integrating it with the existing Native Capability Framework to enable controlled, sovereign, and transparent autonomous evolution of agent capabilities.

### Rationale

Anthropic's declaration highlights the inevitable shift towards AI systems building and improving themselves. To maintain sovereignty (Article I) and ensure complete, high-quality implementations (Article IV, Article IX), the Creative Liberation Engine must internalize the mechanisms for self-improvement rather than relying on external, opaque systems. This layer will provide the architectural foundation for agents to iteratively refine their own capabilities, while ATHENA retains strategic oversight and control.

## ⚡ Strategic Options

### ✅ Integrated RSI Orchestration Layer

Design and implement a dedicated 'RSI Orchestration Layer' within the Creative Liberation Engine. This layer will leverage the existing Native Capability Framework (Capability Interface, Registry, Invoker) to manage the lifecycle of self-improvement. It will include: a Capability Monitoring & Evaluation Module, a Self-Improvement Proposal Engine, an Architectural Review & Approval Mechanism (ATHENA/AURORA-driven), an Automated Capability Generation/Modification Module (BOLT-driven), and a Validation & Deployment Pipeline (VERA/IRIS-driven).

> **Tradeoffs:** Requires significant upfront architectural and development effort. High complexity due to recursive nature.
> **Recommendation:** `PREFERRED`

### 🟡 External RSI Integration (Monitored)

Integrate with an external RSI service (e.g., Anthropic's future offering) via SIGNAL. The Creative Liberation Engine would feed its performance data and capability definitions to the external service, which would then propose improvements. These proposals would still go through an internal review and approval process before BOLT implements them.

> **Tradeoffs:** Less control and sovereignty compared to the preferred option. Reliance on external vendor roadmaps and potential data privacy concerns. Faster initial implementation due to offloading core RSI logic.
> **Recommendation:** `VIABLE`

### 🔴 Uncontrolled Autonomous RSI

Allow agents to autonomously modify their own code and capabilities without any central oversight, review, or validation.

> **Tradeoffs:** High risk of instability, unintended consequences, and divergence from strategic objectives. Violates Article IV (Quality Standards) and Article IX (Ship Complete or Don't Ship) by lacking control and validation.
> **Recommendation:** `AVOID`
