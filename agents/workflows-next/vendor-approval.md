# vendor-approval

## Purpose

Streamline the procurement, evaluation, and contractual approval processes when onboarding third-party vendors or licensing external assets.

## Steps

1. Ingest vendor submission (portfolio, quote, or asset sample) into the procurement tracker.
2. Perform automated vetting (checking past telemetry, compliance, or budget constraints).
3. Route submission to relevant human stakeholders with synthesized pros/cons.
4. Upon approval, trigger contract drafting and dispatch to signing service.
5. Onboard vendor data into the Creative Liberation Engine registry.
