# Helix: Content Creation

> Parent: [IPSV-SPINE.md](./IPSV-SPINE.md)
> Helix ID: `helix-content`

---

## IDEATE

### Outputs
- Topic clusters and angles explored
- References and prior art gathered
- Target audience and intent defined
- Voice / tone direction confirmed
- Content format decided (article, essay, social, video script, etc.)

### Questions to Resolve
- What is the content goal? (inform, persuade, entertain, document)
- Who is the audience? (developers, creatives, investors, general public)
- What platform/channel? (blog, social, newsletter, documentation)
- What voice? (technical, conversational, editorial, poetic)
- Does this tie into a campaign or is it standalone?

---

## PLAN

### Outputs
- Content brief: thesis, audience, key points, CTA
- Outline with section structure
- Calendar slot or publish target date
- Asset requirements (images, diagrams, code samples)
- Research links and source material

### Constraints
- Never draft without a brief
- Always define the single takeaway before writing
- If part of a campaign, reference helix-marketing plan

---

## SHIP

### Phases
1. **First draft** — Write against the outline. Prioritize clarity over polish.
2. **Self-edit** — Cut 20%. Tighten voice. Check flow.
3. **Asset integration** — Add images, code blocks, diagrams.
4. **Final pass** — Proofread. Check links. Verify CTA.
5. **Publish** — Deploy to target platform.

### Outputs
- Final content artifact (markdown, HTML, social post, etc.)
- Published URL or committed file
- Distribution notes (where else this gets shared)

---

## VALIDATE

### Checks
- **Brief alignment:** Does the final piece deliver on the original thesis?
- **Clarity:** Is the single takeaway obvious within 30 seconds?
- **Voice consistency:** Does it match the approved tone?
- **Engagement potential:** Would the target audience share/save this?
- **Reuse value:** Can any section become a standalone asset?

### Learning Log
- What angles resonated vs. fell flat?
- Was the outline sufficient or did the piece evolve significantly?
- What should be reused as a template for similar content?
