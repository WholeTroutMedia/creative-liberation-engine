# Helix: Photography & Visual

> Helix ID: `helix-photo`

---

## IDEATE

### Outputs
- Mood / story / narrative direction
- Reference pulls (existing work, inspiration, visual language)
- Subject matter and emotional intent defined
- Technical approach (camera, lens, lighting, location vs. studio)
- Relationship to broader project (portfolio, campaign, product)

### Questions to Resolve
- What story are we telling with these images?
- Who is the audience? Where will they see this?
- What's the shoot context? (location, studio, event, street)
- What gear/equipment is available?
- What post-production style? (clean, cinematic, editorial, raw)

---

## PLAN

### Outputs
- Shot list with descriptions and priority
- Location scouting / schedule
- Gear checklist
- Lighting plan (if studio)
- Post-production pipeline: culling > selects > edit > export

### Constraints
- Always have a shot list before shooting
- Define select criteria upfront (what makes a keeper?)
- If part of a campaign, reference helix-marketing brief for visual alignment

---

## SHIP

### Phases
1. **Capture** — Execute shot list. Shoot beyond the list when inspiration strikes.
2. **Cull** — First pass: eliminate obvious misses. Second pass: narrow to selects.
3. **Edit** — Color grade, retouch, crop to final quality.
4. **Export** — Multiple formats per destination (web, print, social, portfolio).
5. **Archive** — RAW files to VAULT. Selects tagged and cataloged.

### Outputs
- Final selects exported in required formats
- Images committed to repo or uploaded to target platform
- VAULT archive with RAW originals
- Metadata: EXIF preserved, tags applied

---

## VALIDATE

### Checks
- **Narrative strength:** Do the selects tell the intended story?
- **Technical quality:** Sharpness, exposure, color accuracy
- **Consistency:** Does the set feel cohesive?
- **Usage fit:** Do exports match destination requirements?
- **Catalog hygiene:** Are files properly archived, tagged, and findable?

### Learning Log
- What worked on the shot list vs. what was discovered on set?
- Which editing decisions should become presets?
- What should the next shoot do differently?
- Were there standout images that redefine the visual direction?
