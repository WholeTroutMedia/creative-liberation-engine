# Helix: Marketing & Campaigns

> Parent: [IPSV-SPINE.md](./IPSV-SPINE.md)
> Helix ID: `helix-marketing`

---

## IDEATE

### Outputs
- Campaign concepts (2–3 directions)
- Value propositions articulated
- Target audience segments defined
- Competitive landscape noted
- Channel candidates identified (social, email, paid, organic, PR)

### Questions to Resolve
- What are we selling / launching / announcing?
- Who is the primary audience? Secondary?
- What is the desired action? (sign up, buy, share, attend)
- What budget/resource constraints exist?
- What existing brand assets can we leverage?

---

## PLAN

### Outputs
- Campaign brief: objective, audience, message, channels, timeline
- Channel plan with message variants per surface
- Budget allocation (if applicable)
- Asset list: what needs to be created (copy, visuals, video, landing pages)
- Success metrics defined upfront (conversion, CAC, reach, engagement)

### Constraints
- Every campaign needs a single core message that adapts per channel
- Never launch without tracking/analytics in place
- If assets require design, spawn helix-stitch or helix-photo in parallel

---

## SHIP

### Phases
1. **Asset creation** — Copy, visuals, landing pages, email sequences
2. **Pre-launch check** — All tracking live, all assets reviewed, all links working
3. **Launch** — Deploy across channels per the plan
4. **Optimization** — Monitor first 48h, adjust bids/copy/targeting as needed

### Outputs
- All campaign assets live and tracked
- Launch checklist completed
- Initial performance snapshot (24h / 48h)

---

## VALIDATE

### Checks
- **Performance vs targets:** Conversion rate, CAC, reach, engagement
- **Message resonance:** Which variants performed best? Why?
- **Channel effectiveness:** ROI per channel
- **Asset quality:** Did creative hold up? Any fatigue signals?
- **Learning transfer:** What should inform the next campaign?

### Learning Log
- Best performing message/angle
- Channel surprises (over/under performance)
- Asset production lessons (what took too long, what was reusable)
- Audience insights discovered
