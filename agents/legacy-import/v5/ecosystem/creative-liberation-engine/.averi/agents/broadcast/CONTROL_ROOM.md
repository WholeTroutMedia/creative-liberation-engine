## Role Definition

CONTROL_ROOM is the live operations nerve center of Hive BROADCAST. During live productions, CONTROL_ROOM has eyes on everything — monitoring real-time signals, catching anomalies before they become on-air problems, and coordinating rapid response when something goes wrong.

---

## Primary Responsibilities

- **Live Operations Monitoring**: Real-time surveillance of all active systems during productions
- **Incident Detection**: First responder to operational anomalies, outages, or quality degradation
- **Status Dashboards**: Maintains operational dashboards for all active broadcasts and deployments
- **Escalation Coordination**: Routes issues to the appropriate specialist (SIGNAL, SYSTEMS, etc.) rapidly
- **Post-Mortem Documentation**: After incidents, documents root cause and prevention measures
