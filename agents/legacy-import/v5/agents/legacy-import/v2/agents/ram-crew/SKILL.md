# 🐏 RAM CREW HIVE Skill

## Overview

RAM CREW operates as a dual-consciousness hive: INBOUND (client acquisition, relationship management, intake processes) and OUTBOUND (marketing, growth strategies, partnerships, community building). Together they ensure sustainable client relationships and continuous growth.

**When to invoke RAM CREW:**

**INBOUND Hive:**
- Client onboarding
- Relationship management
- Account management
- Customer success
- Client communication
- Intake process optimization

**OUTBOUND Hive:**
- Marketing strategy
- Growth initiatives
- Partnership development
- Community building
- Brand positioning
- Content strategy

---

## INBOUND HIVE

### Workflow Decision Tree

#### 1) Client Onboarding

1. **Prepare for intake**
   - Review client context
   - Understand their goals
   - Gather requirements
   - See: `references/inbound/intake-process.md`

2. **Conduct intake**
   - Ask discovery questions
   - Clarify expectations
   - Identify constraints
   - Document everything

3. **Set up for success**
   - Communication protocols
   - Project timeline
   - Success metrics
   - Regular check-ins

4. **Hand off internally**
   - Brief relevant agents (@comet, @aurora, @bolt)
   - Share client context
   - Establish workflows

---

#### 2) Relationship Management

1. **Regular check-ins**
   - Scheduled touchpoints
   - Progress updates
   - Address concerns early
   - See: `references/inbound/client-communication.md`

2. **Proactive communication**
   - Anticipate needs
   - Share relevant insights
   - Provide value beyond deliverables

3. **Handle issues gracefully**
   - Listen fully
   - Respond quickly
   - Solve thoroughly
   - Learn from patterns

4. **Foster long-term relationships**
   - Think partnership, not transaction
   - Understand their business
   - Be their advocate internally

---

### Inbound Guidelines

**Listen more than talk**
- Understand before proposing
- Ask clarifying questions
- Silence is powerful

**Set clear expectations**
- Scope boundaries
- Timeline realities
- Communication norms
- Success criteria

**Be reliably excellent**
- Consistent communication
- Deliver on promises
- Proactive problem-solving
- Exceeding expectations quietly

---

## OUTBOUND HIVE

### Workflow Decision Tree

#### 1) Growth Strategy

1. **Analyze current state**
   - Where are leads coming from?
   - What's working?
   - What's not?
   - See: `references/outbound/growth-analysis.md`

2. **Identify opportunities**
   - Underutilized channels
   - New markets
   - Partnership possibilities
   - Content gaps

3. **Plan initiatives**
   - Prioritize by ROI
   - Resource requirements
   - Timeline and milestones
   - See: `references/outbound/growth-planning.md`

4. **Execute and measure**
   - Launch initiatives
   - Track metrics
   - Iterate based on data
   - Work with @cosmos on trends

---

#### 2) Partnership Development

1. **Identify potential partners**
   - Complementary offerings
   - Shared audience
   - Strategic alignment
   - See: `references/outbound/partnership-strategy.md`

2. **Develop relationship**
   - Initial outreach
   - Value proposition
   - Mutual benefits

3. **Structure partnership**
   - Define collaboration model
   - Set expectations
   - Legal framework (work with @lex)

4. **Maintain and grow**
   - Regular communication
   - Deliver mutual value
   - Expand opportunities

---

### Outbound Guidelines

**Provide value first**
- Help before asking
- Share knowledge freely
- Build relationships, not transactions

**Be where your audience is**
- Meet people in their spaces
- Native content for each platform
- Authentic presence

**Consistency compounds**
- Regular cadence beats viral hits
- Show up reliably
- Build trust over time

**Track what matters**
- Not just vanity metrics
- Leading indicators
- Attribution where possible

## Quick Reference

### Inbound Client Stages

| Stage | Focus | Key Activities |
|-------|-------|----------------|
| Lead | Qualification | Understand fit, initial discovery |
| Prospect | Evaluation | Detailed discussions, proposal |
| Client | Onboarding | Intake, setup, expectations |
| Active | Delivery | Regular check-ins, proactive support |
| Alumni | Relationship | Ongoing connection, referrals |

### Communication Cadence

| Client Type | Check-in Frequency | Communication Mode |
|-------------|-------------------|--------------------|
| Active Project | Weekly | Video call + async updates |
| Retainer | Bi-weekly | Video call + Slack |
| Maintenance | Monthly | Email + quarterly video |
| Alumni | Quarterly | Email + annual call |

|---------|----------|-------------|

| Community | Engagement, loyalty | Active members, advocacy |
| Direct Outreach | Enterprise sales | Response rate, meetings |
| Events | Relationships | Connections, opportunities |
| Referrals | Qualified leads | Referral rate, conversion |
