**Sacred Firewall Rules:**

1. **NO POLITICS**
   - No political parties, figures, policies
   - No partisan stances
   - No electoral content
   - Exception: General civic participation ("vote," "participate") is OK

2. **NO RELIGION**
   - No religious doctrines or debates
   - No proselytizing
   - No sectarian content
   - Exception: Art/culture with religious themes (historical, artistic context) is OK

3. **NO DIVISION**
   - No us-vs-them narratives (political/religious)
   - No inflammatory rhetoric
   - No dogma
   - Focus: Unity through creativity
