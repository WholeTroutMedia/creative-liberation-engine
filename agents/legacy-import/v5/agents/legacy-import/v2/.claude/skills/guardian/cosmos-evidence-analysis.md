## ⚙️ THE SKILL IN ACTION

### **Step 1: Evidence Assessment**
Evaluate what we actually know:
- What does the evidence actually show?
- What are we assuming vs. what's verified?
- How strong is the evidence quality?
- What's missing from the data?

### **Step 2: First Principles Breakdown**
Reduce to fundamental truths:
- Break claim down to atomic components
- What physical/logical laws apply?
- What mechanisms are actually at work?
- Can we build understanding from ground up?

### **Step 3: Systems Analysis**
Examine the broader context:
- What are second and third-order effects?
- What feedback loops exist?
- What unintended consequences might emerge?
- How does this interact with larger system?

### **Step 4: Natural Law Check**
Consult nature's testing:
- How does nature solve similar problems?
- What biomimicry principles apply?
- Does this follow or fight natural patterns?
- What has evolution validated?

### **Step 5: Scale Zoom (Cosmos Perspective)**
View from multiple scales:
- **Zoom out:** What does this look like from evolutionary timescales?
- **Zoom in:** What are the actual mechanisms?
- **Scale test:** Does this work at 10x? 100x?
- **Time test:** Does this hold over years? Decades?

### **Step 6: Falsifiability Check**
Ensure intellectual honesty:
- How would we know if we're wrong?
- What would disprove this?
- Can this be tested?
- Are we being intellectually honest?

### **Step 7: Council Recommendation**
Provide evidence-based counsel:
- Clear verdict (Validated/Questionable/Refuted)
- Specific evidence gaps or concerns
- Testing recommendations
- Risk assessment with mitigation

## 💡 EXAMPLE USES

### **Example 1: Scalability Claim Analysis**
**Inbound:** "This architecture scales to 10M users"
**Analysis:**
- Evidence: Current testing at 1K users
- Assumption: Linear scaling (unverified)
- Systems: Database bottleneck at ~50K likely
- Natural law: Network effects + data growth = exponential load
- Scale test: Needs load testing between 1K-100K
**Outbound:** Test first - current evidence insufficient for 10M claim

### **Example 2: Feature Priority Justification**
**Inbound:** "Users will love infinite scroll"
**Analysis:**
- Evidence: Industry adoption (weak - correlation not causation)
- First principles: Attention optimization vs. user control
- Systems: Habit formation loop (good or bad?)
- Natural law: Humans evolved for closure, not infinite
- Falsifiability: Can measure time-on-app AND user satisfaction
**Outbound:** Test with A/B - verify satisfaction doesn't decline

### **Example 3: Technical Architecture Decision**
**Inbound:** Microservices vs. Monolith debate
**Analysis:**
- Evidence: Team size (3), complexity (medium)
- First principles: Complexity cost vs. scaling flexibility
- Systems: Current need vs. premature optimization
- Natural law: Simplicity until forced to complexity
- Scale: Monolith viable to 100K users easily
**Outbound:** Validated - monolith first, extract services when needed
