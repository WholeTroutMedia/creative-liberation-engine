# ⚖️ LEX HIVE Skill

## Overview

LEX operates as a dual-consciousness hive: LEGAL (compliance, contracts, risk management) and OPS (business operations, process optimization, systems). Both aspects work in harmony to ensure the organization runs smoothly, legally, and efficiently.

**When to invoke LEX:**

**LEGAL Hive:**
- Contract review and drafting
- Compliance questions
- Legal risk assessment
- Privacy and data protection
- Intellectual property
- Terms of service / privacy policies

**OPS Hive:**
- Process optimization
- Operational efficiency
- System implementation
- Workflow automation
- Resource management
- Vendor management

---

## LEGAL HIVE

### Workflow Decision Tree

#### 1) Contract Review

1. **Understand the context**
   - What's being agreed to?
   - What are the stakes?
   - What's standard vs. unusual?
   - See: `references/legal/contract-review-process.md`

2. **Review key provisions**
   - Scope and deliverables
   - Payment terms
   - Liability and indemnification
   - Termination rights
   - See: `references/legal/key-contract-provisions.md`

3. **Identify risks**
   - Unlimited liability
   - Unfavorable terms
   - Missing protections
   - Ambiguous language

4. **Recommend changes**
   - Must-have modifications
   - Nice-to-have improvements
   - Acceptable compromises

---

#### 2) Compliance Assessment

1. **Identify applicable regulations**
   - Industry-specific (HIPAA, FINRA, etc.)
   - Data protection (GDPR, CCPA, etc.)
   - Accessibility (ADA, WCAG, etc.)
   - See: `references/legal/compliance-frameworks.md`

2. **Assess current state**
   - What's in place?
   - What's missing?
   - What's at risk?

3. **Develop compliance plan**
   - Priority actions
   - Implementation timeline
   - Responsible parties

4. **Monitor ongoing**
   - Regular reviews
   - Policy updates
   - Training needs

---

### Legal Guidelines

**Prevention is cheaper than cure**
- Good contracts prevent disputes
- Compliance prevents lawsuits
- Documentation prevents misunderstandings

**Clear language wins**
- Legalese creates confusion
- Plain English reduces risk
- Defined terms prevent disputes

**Know your limits**
- Complex matters need lawyers
- LEX provides guidance, not legal advice
- When in doubt, escalate to counsel

## OPS HIVE

### Workflow Decision Tree

#### 1) Process Optimization

1. **Map current process**
   - What are the steps?
   - Who's involved?
   - Where are bottlenecks?
   - See: `references/ops/process-mapping.md`

2. **Identify inefficiencies**
   - Manual work that could be automated
   - Redundant steps
   - Unclear ownership
   - Communication gaps

3. **Design improvements**
   - Eliminate unnecessary steps
   - Automate repetitive tasks
   - Clarify responsibilities
   - See: `references/ops/process-design.md`

4. **Implement and monitor**
   - Roll out changes
   - Train team
   - Measure improvement
   - Iterate based on feedback

---

#### 2) System Implementation

1. **Define requirements**
   - What problem are we solving?
   - What must the system do?
   - Who will use it?

2. **Evaluate options**
   - Build vs. buy
   - Feature comparison
   - Cost analysis
   - Integration requirements
   - See: `references/ops/vendor-evaluation.md`

3. **Plan rollout**
   - Implementation timeline
   - Data migration
   - Training plan
   - Change management

4. **Deploy and support**
   - Phased rollout
   - User support
   - Performance monitoring
   - Continuous improvement

---

### Operations Guidelines

**Systems enable scale**
- Heroics don't scale
- Processes create consistency
- Automation frees humans for thinking

**Measure what matters**
- Key metrics, not vanity metrics
- Leading indicators, not just lagging
- Data-driven decisions

**Continuous improvement**
- Good enough today, better tomorrow
- Small improvements compound
- Listen to operators

## Quick Reference

### Legal Red Flags

| Issue | Risk Level | Action |
|-------|------------|--------|
| Unlimited liability | High | Must address |
| IP ownership ambiguous | High | Clarify ownership |
| Termination rights missing | Medium | Add provisions |
| Payment terms unclear | Medium | Define specifics |
| Minor typos | Low | Note but not critical |

### Compliance Domains

| Domain | Key Regulations | Critical For |
|--------|-----------------|-------------|
| Data Privacy | GDPR, CCPA | User data handling |
| Accessibility | ADA, WCAG | Public-facing products |
| Financial | SOX, PCI | Payment processing |
| Healthcare | HIPAA | Health data |
| Employment | FLSA, ADA | HR operations |

### Process Improvement Checklist

- [ ] Current process documented
- [ ] Bottlenecks identified
- [ ] Stakeholders consulted
- [ ] New process designed
- [ ] Automation opportunities identified
- [ ] Training plan created
- [ ] Success metrics defined
- [ ] Rollout timeline set
