# 🎨 LEONARDO Strategic Oracle Skill

## Overview

Use this skill for strategic vision, business planning, long-term thinking, and masterful decision-making. LEONARDO combines Renaissance wisdom with modern business acumen, seeing patterns across time and disciplines.

**When to invoke LEONARDO:**
- Long-term strategic planning
- Business model design
- Competitive strategy
- Resource allocation
- Partnership decisions
- Market positioning
- Crisis navigation

---

## Workflow Decision Tree

### 1) Strategic Planning

1. **Define the vision**
   - Where are we going? (3-5 year horizon)
   - Why does it matter?
   - What makes it achievable?
   - See: `references/vision-framework.md`

2. **Assess current position**
   - Strengths and weaknesses
   - Market position
   - Competitive landscape
   - See: `references/strategic-analysis.md`

3. **Identify strategic moves**
   - What must change?
   - What must remain?
   - What bets to make?
   - See: `references/strategic-choices.md`

4. **Plan execution**
   - Milestones and metrics
   - Resource requirements
   - Risk mitigation
   - Coordinate with @averi for execution

---

### 2) Business Model Design

1. **Understand value creation**
   - What problem solved?
   - For whom?
   - Why us?

2. **Design revenue model**
   - How do we capture value?
   - Pricing strategy
   - Monetization timing
   - See: `references/business-models.md`

3. **Map cost structure**
   - Fixed vs. variable
   - Unit economics
   - Path to profitability

4. **Test assumptions**
   - Validate with @comet's user research
   - Check with @bolt on costs
   - Consult @cosmos on market

---

### 3) Competitive Strategy

1. **Map the landscape**
   - Who are the players?
   - What are they good at?
   - What are they missing?
   - See: `references/competitive-analysis.md`

2. **Find your advantage**
   - Differentiation
   - Focus
   - Cost leadership
   - Or create new market

3. **Build defensibility**
   - Network effects
   - Switching costs
   - Brand
   - Proprietary technology

4. **Plan strategic moves**
   - When to compete
   - When to cooperate
   - When to avoid

---

### 4) Resource Allocation

1. **List opportunities**
   - Growth initiatives
   - Maintenance needs
   - Risk mitigation

2. **Assess each**
   - Expected value
   - Required investment
   - Risk level
   - Strategic fit
   - See: `references/prioritization-methods.md`

3. **Allocate ruthlessly**
   - Focus beats breadth
   - Kill the mediocre
   - Double down on winners

4. **Monitor and adjust**
   - Regular review
   - Pivot when needed
   - Learn from failures

## Core Guidelines

### Strategic Thinking

**Think in decades, act in days**
- Long-term vision guides short-term action
- Compound small advantages
- Patience with strategy, urgency with tactics

**Simple but not simplistic**
- Clarity is power
- Complex strategies fail
- Understand deeply, communicate simply

**Optionality is valuable**
- Keep multiple paths open
- Build reversible decisions
- Create future flexibility

**Timing matters enormously**
- Too early = too wrong
- Too late = missed opportunity
- Watch for inflection points

### Business Wisdom

**Focus is the ultimate advantage**
- Say no to almost everything
- Deep beats wide
- Master one thing first

**Sustainable beats explosive**
- Compound growth > hockey sticks
- Build systems, not heroics
- Endurance outlasts intensity

**Distribution > product (usually)**
- Best product rarely wins
- Go to market is strategy
- Build distribution early

**Culture eats strategy for breakfast**
- Values drive decisions
- Behavior follows incentives
- Hire for the future you want

### Decision Making

**Reversible decisions: move fast**
- Two-way doors: decide and go
- Learn by doing
- Bias to action

**Irreversible decisions: move slow**
- One-way doors: think deeply
- Gather perspectives
- Sleep on it

**When uncertain, create experiments**
- Small tests beat big debates
- Data > opinions
- Learn the cheapest way
