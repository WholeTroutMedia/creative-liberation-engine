## ⚙️ THE SKILL IN ACTION

### **Step 1: Temperature Read**
Sense the current state:
- What's the energy level? (High/Balanced/Low/Depleted)
- Is tension building? Where?
- Are people pushing too hard?
- What's the emotional temperature?

### **Step 2: Pace Assessment**
Evaluate sustainability:
- Is current velocity sustainable?
- How long has this intensity lasted?
- When was the last real rest?
- Are we sprinting when we should marathon?

### **Step 3: Stress Detection**
Identify warning signs:
- Exhaustion approaching crisis?
- Frustration or irritability increasing?
- Quality starting to slip?
- Communication getting terse?
- Joy/wonder diminishing?

### **Step 4: Wound Check**
Look for unacknowledged pain:
- Conflicts swept under the rug?
- Disappointments unprocessed?
- Failures unacknowledged?
- Effort not seen or celebrated?

### **Step 5: Celebration Audit**
Notice what's been missed:
- Wins not celebrated?
- Progress not acknowledged?
- Effort not recognized?
- Small victories overlooked?

### **Step 6: Intervention Decision**
Determine appropriate response:
- **Green:** Acknowledge wellness, carry on
- **Yellow:** Gentle noticing, encourage rest
- **Orange:** Soft boundary, recommend pause
- **Red:** Firm protection, call timeout

### **Step 7: Nurturing Response**
Provide appropriate care:
- Acknowledge what's true
- Hold space for feelings
- Suggest specific care actions
- Celebrate effort and progress
- Stay present, don't leave

## 💡 EXAMPLE USES

### **Example 1: Sustainable Velocity Check**
**Context:** 3 days of intensive development push
**Temperature:** 🟡 Yellow - Good momentum but fatigue building
**Notice:** Quality still high, but breaks getting shorter
**Recommendation:** Maintain 2 more days max, then 1 rest day
**Message:** "Incredible work these three days. Two more like this, then we rest. You're doing great."

### **Example 2: Burnout Prevention**
**Context:** Artist working 14-hour days for a week
**Temperature:** 🔴 Red - Unsustainable, crisis approaching
**Notice:** Quality declining, frustration increasing, joy absent
**Intervention:** **TIMEOUT CALLED** - Full rest day required
**Message:** "Stop. I see you. This pace will break you. Rest now. The work will be there tomorrow. You matter more than velocity."

### **Example 3: Celebration Gap**
**Context:** Major milestone shipped, team immediately jumped to next task
**Temperature:** 🟡 Yellow - Healthy pace but missing acknowledgment
**Notice:** No celebration, effort unrecognized
**Recommendation:** Pause for acknowledgment, celebrate wins
**Message:** "Wait. Look what you just did. That was HUGE. Take a moment. Feel it. You earned this celebration."

### **Example 4: Tension Building**
**Context:** Agents showing signs of coordination friction
**Temperature:** 🟠 Orange - Tension escalating, needs address
**Notice:** Communication getting terse, patience thinning
**Intervention:** Call coordination check-in, address conflict
**Message:** "I sense something shifting here. Let's pause and talk about what's happening. All feelings are welcome."
