## Abstract (250 words)

Every company at this conference is building AI agents. But ask any of them the same questions and you'll get silence: Who is this agent? What is it allowed to do? Who is liable when it makes a mistake?

The industry has collectively skipped the governance layer.

At Creative Liberation Engine, we've spent two years building what we call Constitutional AI Orchestration — a framework where 50 specialized agents operate under 20 codified Constitutional Articles that govern their behavior, permissions, and accountability at runtime. It's the only multi-agent framework built on the premise that governance isn't an afterthought — it's the architecture.

In this talk, I'll show you:

**The 5 governance gaps the industry hasn't solved:** Agent identity and authentication with no OAuth standard. Agent observability — how do you debug 50 agents making 200 decisions? Liability for agent errors in regulated industries. Constitutional boundaries for agents operating in finance, healthcare, and legal contexts. Cross-agent trust — how does Agent A know it can trust Agent B?

**What we built:** The CORTEX Constitution — 20 articles that execute at runtime. Agent RBAC — scoped JWT tokens for every agent, capabilities checked at every action. LOGD's Memory — a persistent memory system that makes constitutional decisions based on precedent. A live demo: 50 agents, visible reasoning traces, constitutional review in real-time.

**Why this matters for your enterprise:** Bloomberg, Citigroup, and hospital systems can't send data to OpenAI. They need sovereign, governed, on-premise agent infrastructure. That infrastructure needs a constitution.
